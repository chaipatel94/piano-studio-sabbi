FAQ

Do I need a piano?

A keyboard works to start. Preferably 61+ keys (88 keys is best long-term).

How often should I take lessons?

Most students progress best with weekly lessons. Twice a week can be great early on.

What age do you teach?

Typically ages 6+ through adults. Younger students may need shorter lessons.

Can you teach songs I like?

Yes — we mix fundamentals with music you enjoy (pop, classical, film, etc.).
